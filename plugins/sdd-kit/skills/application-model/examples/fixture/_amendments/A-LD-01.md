# A-LD-01 — the landing table gives a row to the primary actor only

**Where the standard could not be followed.** The standard for one use case, section 13.1, gives a
landing row to the goal's primary actor. Extension 3a of UC-LD-02 calls on a second actor, the
head librarian, who records the condition of a damaged item in the librarian's place. No row of
section 13.1 or 13.2, and none of section 16 of the standard for the screens, lands an actor other
than the primary actor.

**What was done.** The head librarian was landed as a role of the model, given the return form's
permission, as a judgement written down as an assumption in the ledger's `outside` list, and the
model's check that every role it names is declared was left to decide the rest.

**Who is to answer.** The owner of the standard for one use case, at its next edition: a row for
an actor of the use case model's catalogue other than the primary actor, landing among the model's
roles.

*A fixture of the method's application-model skill; the lending desk of Eastbrook does not exist.*
