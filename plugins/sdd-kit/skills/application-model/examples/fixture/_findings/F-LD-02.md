# F-LD-02 — the screens carry no group of screens

**What was missing.** The model's navigation groups its screens in categories with the roles
allowed. The standard for the screens, section 16.3, says the groups of screens are written once
for the whole application in a part of the screens the standard does not yet carry, and the two
screen records carry none.

**Where the model needed it.** The model's `navigation`, whose menus the model's check requires to
name what exists.

**What was done.** Two categories were written: the desk, holding the two searches, and a hidden
category holding the three forms a choice opens, so that none stands on a visible menu, as the
interaction design's Acts table decides. The ledger records them as an assumption in its `outside`
list.

**Who is to answer.** The analyst who wrote the screens, once the standard for the screens carries
the part written once for the whole application.
