which disagree in two places.*

| Reference | What it is | Authority | Date and version | Status | Overlap | Absorption |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I-1 | The town's allotment bylaw | the town council | 2026, as amended | current | I-2, I-3 | fully absorbed |
| I-2 | The garden committee's working notes | the garden committee | 2026 | current | I-1 | partly absorbed — the notes on plot sizes belong to no use case |
| I-3 | The old allocation spreadsheet's column list | the former garden officer | undated | superseded | I-1 | not absorbed — superseded by I-1; kept for the reason CF-2 records |

The precedence order: I-1 first, as the rule the registry must not contradict; I-2 where I-1
is silent; I-3 only where neither speaks.

residents and their households) and CF-2 (the order of allocation: the bylaw says the order of
acceptance, the spreadsheet sorted by plot size).
