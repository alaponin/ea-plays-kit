# The finding rubric

## Damage order

Rank each finding by what a reviewer would do with it. The rank orders the table.

| Rank | Kind of finding | Why it ranks here | The question shape |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | An exchange the framework carries, or intends to carry in the first wave, with **no authorising clause** | the exchange is unlawful the day it goes live | *Does clause [x] reach exchange [n], or does [n] leave the wave until the decree is amended?* |
| 2 | An article that **authorises an exchange the catalogue does not carry** and that a principle would not permit (sharing beyond purpose) | a reviewer reads it as a grant of general sharing | *Is the broader authority deliberate? If so, which principle justifies it; if not, which clause narrows it?* |
| 3 | A **standard at a different version** in the decree, the portfolio or the register — or one the decree references that the portfolio does not carry | every member must match the portfolio exactly; two versions means no conformance test can pass | *Which version is binding on [date], and which document changes?* (cite the standard's own publication beside the question) |
| 4 | A **role or body** the RACI or the decree names that the Governance Pack does not describe, or the reverse | a decision with no Accountable, or an Accountable with no mandate | *Which document is current — does the body exist, and under what mandate?* |
| 5 | A **service implemented with no decree authorisation**, or authorised with no catalogued service | the bus carries what the law does not permit, or the law permits what nobody built | *Is the service in scope? If yes, which article; if no, why is it on the bus?* |
| 6 | A **regulator/operator merge** — one document gives the operating authority a power that B14 reserved to the regulator | Module 3's split collapses | *Which body holds power [p] — and does the other document need to change?* |
| 7 | A **key term used inconsistently** | a court or a member reads the term in the wrong document | *Which definition governs, and where is it stated once?* |

## The terms that drift most

| Term | The two readings that collide |
| --- | --- |
| member | the organisation admitted to the bus · the subsystem that calls a service |
| service | a business service to a citizen · an X-Road service (a published API) |
| authority | the operating authority · the regulator · any public body |
| authoritative source | the register the decree designates · the register a body happens to hold |
| subsystem | an X-Road subsystem code · an application of a member |
| connection | membership of the bus · a live service registration |
| conformance | a self-assessment · a third-party test · a test suite passed |

## What is not a finding

- A difference of wording that carries no difference of meaning.
- A gap that the document itself acknowledges as `[confirm]`. Report it once, in a
  separate list *Open placeholders carried by the documents*, not as a contradiction.
- Anything about a document the learner did not paste.

## The check on the check

Before you give the report, count: every finding has two passages, two locations, a rank
and a question. A row that lacks one of the five is removed, not padded.
