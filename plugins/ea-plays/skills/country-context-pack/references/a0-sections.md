# The seven A0 sections — exact shape

Each section carries the provenance header if delivered alone; the whole pack carries one
header covering all seven.

## §1 Digital-landscape brief — feeds 1.1, 1.2

Prose, four paragraphs, in this order: (1) connectivity and affordability; (2) the
digital-government strategy in force and the body that owns it; (3) identity and
cross-agency systems — data exchange, payments, civil registration; (4) maturity and gaps
against a published index. Every institution, system and document named with its year.

## §2 Programme list — feeds 1.3, 1.5

| Programme | Lead body | Status | Year | Funder | Budget envelope | Blocks it needs | Source (tier, date) |

*Status:* planned / procurement / live / scaling. *Blocks:* any of identity, payments,
data exchange, registration, consent. Follow the table with two or three cross-cutting
observations — connectivity, financing, sequencing.

## §3 Ministry operating context — feeds 1.4

Prose under seven headings: institutional structure and decentralisation · policy framework
and sector plan · legal constraints · financial constraints (donor dependence, documented
PFM failures) · technical and data-system constraints · human-resource constraints ·
political context. Then one paragraph: **what is absent from the public record**.

## §4 Institutional roles register — feeds 1.6, 1.7

| Post | Institution | Mandate (one line) | Status | Source (tier, date) |

*Status:* confirmed / partial / gap. Then: **Roles an EA programme needs that are missing** —
one line each, naming the phase the absence blocks.

Posts, never names. See `output-contract.md` §2.

## §5 Country characteristics — feeds 1.8, 5.1

One paragraph, no table, covering in order: population · income classification · governance
type and sub-national autonomy · region and regional bodies · digital-government maturity
with index rankings · national ID, civil registration, data exchange, payments · the
coordinating digital body and whether binding · budget-cycle constraints · main donors ·
whether an EA function or board exists.

It is written to be pasted into a comparator search, so it must be dense and specific:
"lower-middle-income, 16.8m, unitary with delegated provinces" beats "a small developing
country".

## §6 Public bodies, systems and registries — feeds 2.1, 2.4, 2.5, 4.1

| Body | Mandate | Systems it runs | Registries it holds | Confidence | Source (tier, date) |

*Confidence:* confirmed / inferred. Follow the table with: **Not found where one would be
expected** — bodies or functions whose absence is itself a finding.

## §7 Legal and policy list — feeds 2.3, 1.7

| Instrument | Year | Status | Owning body | The one constraint it places on an EA design | Source (tier, date) |

*Status:* enacted / bill / draft / repealed / published-but-not-applied.

## What every section carries

- Its own heading, naming the section number and the plays it feeds.
- A source per claim, inline, with tier and access date.
- ⚠ on any line that `cite-or-discard` could not verify.
- A stated absence rather than a silent omission.
