# The seven A0 sections — the exact shape

If you give one section alone, that section carries the provenance header. If you give the
full pack, one header covers all seven sections.

## §1 Digital-landscape brief — feeds 1.1, 1.2

Write four paragraphs of prose in this order: (1) connectivity and affordability; (2) the
digital-government strategy in force, and the body that owns it; (3) identity, and the
systems that cross agencies, which are data exchange, payments and civil registration; (4)
maturity and gaps, against a published index. Give the year with each institution, system
and document.

## §2 Programme list — feeds 1.3, 1.5

| Programme | Lead body | Status | Year | Funder | Budget envelope | Blocks it needs | Source (tier, date) |

The *status* is planned, procurement, live or scaling. The *blocks* are identity, payments,
data exchange, registration and consent. After the table, write two or three observations
that cross the programmes: connectivity, financing and sequence.

## §3 Ministry operating context — feeds 1.4

Write prose under seven headings: institutional structure and decentralisation · policy
framework and sector plan · legal constraints · financial constraints, which include donor
dependence and each PFM failure in the record · constraints in the technology and the data
systems · constraints in human resources · political context. Then write one paragraph:
**what the public record does not contain**.

## §4 Institutional roles register — feeds 1.6, 1.7

| Post | Institution | Mandate (one line) | Status | Source (tier, date) |

The *status* is confirmed, partial or gap. After the table, write **Roles an EA programme
needs that are missing**. Write one line for each role, and name the phase that the absence
blocks.

Posts, never names. See `output-contract.md` §2.

## §5 Country characteristics — feeds 5.1

Write one paragraph. Do not use a table. Give these items in order: population · income
classification · type of governance and sub-national autonomy · region and regional bodies ·
digital-government maturity with the index rankings · the national ID, the civil
registration, the data exchange and the payments · the digital body that coordinates, and
whether its mandate binds · constraints of the budget cycle · main donors · whether an EA
function or board exists.

A learner pastes this paragraph into a comparator search. Therefore it must be dense and
specific. "Lower-middle-income, 16.8m, unitary with delegated provinces" is better than "a
small developing country".

## §6 Public bodies, systems and registries — feeds 2.1, 2.4, 2.5, 4.1

| Body | Mandate | Systems it runs | Registries it holds | Confidence | Source (tier, date) |

The *confidence* is confirmed or inferred. After the table, write **Not found where one
would be expected**. List the bodies and functions whose absence is a finding.

## §7 Legal and policy list — feeds 2.3, 1.7

| Instrument | Year | Status | Owning body | The one constraint it places on an EA design | Source (tier, date) |

The *status* is enacted, bill, draft, repealed, or published but not applied.

## What each section carries

- Its own heading, which gives the section number and the plays that it feeds.
- One source for each claim, in the same line, with the tier and the access date.
- A ⚠ mark on each line that `cite-or-discard` could not verify.
- A statement of each absence. Do not omit an absence in silence.
