# The published models to adapt from

An article of the decree is drafted against a named, published instrument, or it is a
`[confirm]` for counsel. This file names the instruments, says which provision does what,
and says what does not transfer. Open the consolidated text on the official portal each
time. Do not quote a section number from this file; quote it from the text you fetched
today. Section numbers here are pointers to check, not citations to copy.

## Where the models are published (Tier 1)

| Model | Portal | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Estonia — Public Information Act (Avaliku teabe seadus), Chapter 5¹ *Data exchange layer of information systems*, and the Government regulation *Andmevahetuskiht X-tee* (the X-Road regulation) | Riigi Teataja, English translations at riigiteataja.ee/en | mandatory connection; the operator's powers; the duty to use the exchange layer for data from another database; the register of information systems (RIHA) |
| Estonia — Information Society Services Act | Riigi Teataja | the KP2 play pages cite this act for the structure of mandatory connection. Read it, but note that the exchange-layer duty sits in the Public Information Act chapter above. Say which of the two you adapted from. |
| EU — Regulation (EU) 2018/1724, the Single Digital Gateway Regulation, Article 14 *Technical system for the cross-border automated exchange of evidence and application of the once-only principle* | EUR-Lex, eli/reg/2018/1724 | the once-only obligation: evidence exchanged at the user's explicit request, from the authentic source, with a preview |
| EU — the European Interoperability Framework, Commission Communication COM(2017) 134 and its annex | ec.europa.eu / EUR-Lex; joinup.ec.europa.eu | the underlying principles article: adopt the twelve, do not draft fresh; the four layers; interoperability governance |
| EU — Regulation (EU) 2024/903, the Interoperable Europe Act | EUR-Lex | the interoperability assessment duty; a board with a mandate; the sharing of interoperability solutions |
| EU — Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the GDPR, Articles 5 and 6 | EUR-Lex | the data-protection article: purpose limitation, data minimisation, lawful basis for a public body (Art. 6(1)(c) legal obligation, 6(1)(e) public task) |
| Finland — Act on Information Management in Public Administration (906/2019) and Act on Common e-Government Support Services (571/2016) | Finlex, finlex.fi | a second model for mandatory use of a national exchange layer (Suomi.fi Data Exchange Layer) and for information-management duties of a public body |
| GovStack PAERA v1.0 §5.2, principle #5 Once-Only | paera.govstack.global | the principle as the framework states it; cite it beside the EU model |

The country's own instruments come from the legal register (`ea-legal-context`), not from
this file.

## What each component adapts from

| Component | Model | What to take |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explanatory Memorandum | none — it is the country's own case, from B4 | the problem, what the decree does, why now, the expected benefit. Every factual claim needs evidence: cite A0 §8, §9, §10 or B1, or mark ⚠ |
| Preamble | the country's constitution and enabling statutes, from the register | the authority under which the decree is made; each recital is a `[confirm]` unless the register gives it |
| Draft Articles | the table below | one model per article |
| Cover Note | none — a transmittal | what the package contains; the actions requested; the route (decree or primary legislation) as a **question**; the timeline and the cost of delay |
| Two-Track Regulatory Memo | the country's data-protection act, from the register; GDPR Arts. 5–6 as the reference shape | the points of intersection and, for each, defer / fill / conflict |

## What each operative article adapts from

| Article | Model provision | What transfers | What does not transfer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scope and definitions | EIF annex (definitions of interoperability and its layers); the Estonian regulation's definitions of member, service, subsystem | the terms *member*, *service*, *authoritative source*, *exchange* | a definition that refers to an EU institution or a member state |
| Mandatory connection | Estonia, Public Information Act Chapter 5¹: the duty of a database holder to exchange data through the exchange layer; Finland 571/2016 | that designated bodies *shall* connect; that data held by another body is fetched through the layer, not asked of the citizen | the list of bodies — take it from B5's first wave, not from the model |
| Once-only obligation | SDG Regulation Art. 14 | exchange from the authentic source; at the request of the user; the user's right to preview; the record of the exchange | the cross-border machinery and the EU-level technical system; the explicit-request rule may be replaced by a statutory basis for a domestic statutory service — counsel decides |
| Data protection and consent | GDPR Arts. 5 and 6; the country's data-protection act | purpose limitation; minimisation; a lawful basis per exchange; the record of processing | the supervisory structure — the country's DPA is the one in the register, with its real powers, which may be few |
| Principles | EIF twelve underlying principles | adopt them by reference; explain each in one line | none — adoption by reference is the point; drafting fresh principles is the error |
| Operating authority's powers and duties | Estonia's regulation (the operator of the exchange layer); Interoperable Europe Act (the board) | to admit and remove members; to keep the catalogue; to set technical rules; to monitor; to report | the split between regulator and operator is B14's — read it; the article must not merge the two hats that Module 3 separated |
| Dispute and enforcement | no single published model transfers. Use the EIF governance recommendation and the country's administrative procedure act from the register | the enforcement ladder in `component-templates.md`; proportionality; a right of appeal | every sanction — a fine, a restriction, a suspension — is `[confirm]` until counsel confirms the decree can carry it. Many jurisdictions reserve fines to primary legislation |

## The one rule

If you cannot fetch a published model for an article, do not draft the article. Write:
*"No published model was reachable for the [x] article on [date]. The article is not
drafted. Counsel to supply a model or to draft from the country's own precedent."* An
article with no model behind it is worse than a gap.
