# Chain discipline

The difference between running the method and running the method *on a workbook*.

## Read, do not ask

Every phase's input is an artefact with a number. Before asking the learner for anything,
check `workbook-chain.md` for what this phase consumes and ask whether they have it. The
plays' habit of saying "paste your Discovery brief" is what this skill exists to replace.

Where an artefact exists but is thin, use it and say what is thin. Where it does not exist,
name it and the play that produces it, and stop:

> 4.4 needs **A14 rev.2 — Ranked gap analysis**, produced by play 4.3, and the **BB status
> register** from `bb-landscape-check`. I have A23 but not A14 rev.2. Run 4.3 first.

Three things never happen: inventing the missing artefact; substituting a weaker one
silently; proceeding and hoping.

## Write back with provenance

Every deliverable carries the header, and **Consumed** names what was actually read — not
what the chain says should have been read. If A11 was missing and the target architecture
was checked against principles the learner supplied verbally, **Consumed** says so.

That field is how a learner three weeks later knows whether the target architecture was
built on the ranked gaps or on an earlier draft.

## Where the verification skills are called

| Phase | Skill | Why the safeguard demands it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discover (4.2) | `cite-or-discard`, **audit mode** | The brief promises a source column; audit mode is what populates it, line by line |
| Adapt (4.4) | `bb-landscape-check` | *Share* against a block that is planned rather than live is the most expensive error the method can make |
| Plan — target (4.5) | `bb-landscape-check` | Path-to-acquire depends on what exists; re-read rather than trusting the matrix's copy |
| Execute (4.7) | `bb-landscape-check`, re-run | "Authoritative and available" is the gate's substance. A status three months old has already been wrong once. |

Say in the output that the call was made and what it returned. A gate paper that asserts a
block is live without showing the register is the paper the Board sends back.

## Clarifying questions

At most three, asked at once, and answered into the artefact as an *Inputs supplied by the
learner* block. The August 2026 runs showed the tool-selection play asking two questions
before answering and the answers evaporating into the conversation; they belong in the
deliverable, because the next phase depends on them and the learner will not remember.

## Strip the reasoning

The single most common chain break in the test runs, after file output. "The search
confirms… Let me produce it." is the model talking to itself, and it appeared at the top of
a pasted context in the 2.5 run. Nothing precedes the provenance header.

## What the transfer modes reuse

| Reused across sectors | Rebuilt per sector |
| --- | --- |
| A0 §1–§5 and §7 — landscape, characteristics, roles, legal | A0 §6 — the sector's bodies, systems and registries |
| The BB status register — shared blocks are shared | The sector's own registries |
| The method, the phase structure, the sign-off questions | Every gap, every sourcing call, every wave |
| A11 principles | The target architecture |

Rebuilding A0 §6 for the new sector is `ea-institution-mapper`'s job. Reusing the rest is
the whole argument of 4.8 and 5.3 — say so in the output, because it is the finding the
minister cares about: the second sector costs less than the first.
