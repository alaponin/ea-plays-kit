# Chain discipline

Chain discipline is the difference between a run of the method and a run of the method *on
a workbook*.

## Read the workbook. Do not ask the learner.

The input to each phase is an artefact with a number. Before you ask the learner for
anything, read `workbook-chain.md` for what this phase consumes. Then ask the learner
whether they have that artefact. The plays say "paste your Discovery brief". This skill
exists to replace that habit.

If an artefact exists and it is thin, use it, and say what is thin. If an artefact does not
exist, name it and name the play that makes it. Then stop:

> 4.4 needs **A14 rev.2 — Ranked gap analysis**, produced by play 4.3, and the **BB status
> register** from `bb-landscape-check`. I have A23 but not A14 rev.2. Run 4.3 first.

Three things never happen. You never invent the artefact that is missing. You never put a
weaker artefact in its place in silence. You never continue and hope.

## Write back with the provenance

Each deliverable carries the header. The **Consumed** field names what you read. It does not
name what the chain says that you must read. If A11 was missing, and you checked the target
architecture against principles that the learner gave you in the conversation, then
**Consumed** says that.

Three weeks later, a learner reads that field to know whether the target architecture came
from the ranked gaps or from an earlier draft.

## Where to call the verification skills

| Phase | Skill | Why the safeguard needs it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discover (4.2) | `cite-or-discard`, in **audit mode** | The brief promises a source column. Audit mode fills that column, line by line |
| Adapt (4.4) | `bb-landscape-check` | To *share* against a block that is planned and not live is the most expensive error in the method |
| Plan — target (4.5) | `bb-landscape-check` | The path to acquire an element depends on what exists. Read the register again. Do not trust the copy in the matrix |
| Execute (4.7) | `bb-landscape-check`, run again | "Authoritative and available" is the substance of the gate. A status three months old was already wrong one time |

Say in the output that you called the skill, and say what it returned. A Board sends back a
gate paper that says a block is live and does not show the register.

## Questions to the learner

Ask a maximum of three questions. Ask them at the same time. Write the answers into the
artefact in an *Inputs supplied by the learner* block. In the August 2026 runs, the
tool-selection play asked two questions before it answered, and the answers stayed in the
conversation and were then lost. The answers belong in the deliverable. The next phase
depends on them, and the learner will not remember them.

## Remove the analysis

After a file output, this is the most common break in the chain in the test runs. "The
search confirms… Let me produce it." is the model that speaks to itself. It was at the top of
a pasted context in the 2.5 run. Nothing comes before the provenance header.

## What a transfer reuses

| Reused across the sectors | Built again for each sector |
| --- | --- |
| A0 §1 to §5 and §7 — the landscape, the characteristics, the roles, the law | A0 §6 — the bodies, the systems and the registries of the sector |
| The BB status register, because the shared blocks are shared | The registries of the sector |
| The method, the structure of the phases, the sign-off questions | Each gap, each sourcing decision, each wave |
| The A11 principles | The target architecture |

To build A0 §6 again for the new sector is the work of `ea-institution-mapper`. To reuse the
rest is the full argument of plays 4.8 and 5.3. Say so in the output, because it is the
finding that the minister wants: the second sector costs less than the first.
