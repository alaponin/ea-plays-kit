# The principles — where they are published

Adopt by citation. Do not draft a principle fresh; a framework whose principles are its
own has nothing to point to when they are challenged. Fetch the source, quote the line,
add the implication for this country.

| Principle | Source (Tier 1) | How the source states it | The implication shape |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Whole-of-government | GovStack PAERA v1.0 §5.2 (the principles; whole-of-government); EIF underlying principle *openness* and the *integrated public service governance* recommendation | shared infrastructure and shared rules across bodies rather than per-programme builds | *no programme in the first wave procures its own exchange mechanism; each connects to the shared layer* |
| Once-only | PAERA v1.0 §5.2 Principle #5 (Once-Only); EU Regulation 2018/1724 Art. 14; EIF *administrative simplification* | a public body does not ask a person for data the administration already holds and can lawfully fetch | *the first exchange is [B3]; the intake form for [service] loses [n] fields* |
| Reuse-first | PAERA v1.0 (building-block reuse); EIF underlying principle *reusability*; the GovStack building-block approach | reuse an existing block or standard before building; share what is built | *the bus is the existing X-Road deployment, not a new procurement; the semantic map aligns to a published vocabulary* |
| Cross-border readiness | EIF (European Interoperability Framework — the cross-border dimension); PAERA v1.0 §5.2 (cross-border where stated) | design so that an exchange can cross a border later without redesign | *identifiers and vocabularies are the published ones; the transport protocol is the standard one* |

The EIF's twelve underlying principles, for the decree's principles article (Module 2):
subsidiarity and proportionality; openness; transparency; reusability; technological
neutrality and data portability; user-centricity; inclusion and accessibility; security
and privacy; multilingualism; administrative simplification; preservation of information;
assessment of effectiveness and efficiency. Cite the EIF annex; do not paraphrase the list
from memory — fetch it.

## The mandate — where the authority comes from

| Kind of authority | Where to look | If it is not there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| a digital-government or e-government act | the Legal register; A0 §7 | the decree must create the mandate — say so |
| the establishing act of the digital agency | the Legal register; A0 §6 | a coordinating mandate that does not bind is not a mandate to require connection |
| a cabinet decision or national strategy | A0 §1, §7 | a draft strategy is not authority; cite it as intent |
| a sector plan | A0 §7 | it authorises the sector, not the whole of government |
