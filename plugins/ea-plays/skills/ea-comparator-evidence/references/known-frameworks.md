# Published national EA frameworks and coordination instruments

**A starting point for search, not a substitute for it.** Every entry must be re-verified
at run time: URLs move, frameworks are superseded, agencies are restructured. Search for
comparators that are *not* on this list — a set drawn only from here is a set drawn from
whatever was well-documented in 2026.

## Contested cases worth knowing about

Search for the current state of each — these move, and a card that reports a 2021 ruling
without its appeal is wrong.

- **Kenya, Huduma Namba** — the litigation over the national ID programme; the tip's own
  example. Look for the judgments, the data-protection impact assessment findings, and the
  successor programme.
- Programmes halted by a **data-protection regulator** or an **audit office** — search the
  regulator's decisions and the auditor-general's reports directly; these are Tier 1 and
  almost never surface in general search.
- **Frameworks published and never applied** — the most common and least reported failure.
  Evidence looks like: a framework document from year *n*, and procurement or project
  documents from year *n+3* that do not reference it. That contrast is a legitimate,
  sourceable finding.

## What a card must not do

Report a country's **plan** as its achievement. Every national digital strategy describes
an architecture; almost none describe one that exists. The card's *what it built* row needs
evidence of operation — a member list, a transaction volume, an audit, an evaluation — not
a chapter heading.
