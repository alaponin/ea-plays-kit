**Produces:** B14 — Owner's mandate, regulator and operator split

## What the learner types

I am defining the Operating Authority for [country X]'s Government Interoperability Framework — the body that will own and run the platform day to day. Here is what I know of the candidate body or bodies [paste: any existing digital-government agency, e-government authority, or ICT unit that could host this role, and its current mandate]. Draft a mandate statement covering: (1) the duties, split into two hats — the REGULATOR (owns the reference architecture and standards, defines the compliance method and evidence, grants written exceptions, applies sanctions) and the OPERATOR (runs the platform, admits and onboards members, validates connections, sets and monitors service levels, supports members) — and state where each hat sits (two agencies, or two units inside one agency with separate mandates); (2) the powers each needs (and which it would NOT currently have under the candidate body's existing mandate — flag these as [confirm] gaps to close); (3) the organisational form (dedicated agency / ministry department / independent statutory body) with the trade-off for [country X]; (4) the resources — standing staff roles with an indicative build-up from a small initial team to national scale, and a budget line, not project funding; (5) how both hats relate to the governing tiers (they regulate and operate; the tiers govern). Output: a one-page mandate plus a list of mandate gaps to close.
