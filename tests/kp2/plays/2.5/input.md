## What the learner types

I am transmitting an interoperability decree package to the Ministry of Justice in [country X]. The country's personal-data-protection law is [in force / in parliament / in draft / absent — name it if it exists]. Draft two short documents. (1) COVER NOTE — three short paragraphs: what the package contains; the specific actions requested of the Ministry (review and perfect the Articles, confirm the Preamble authorities, advise on the route to enactment — executive decree versus primary legislation, noting [confirm: whether an enabling law already exists]); and the timeline with the cost of delay. (2) TWO-TRACK REGULATORY MEMO — sections: (a) the two instruments; (b) points of intersection — definitions, consent and data-subject rights, cross-border transfer, the Data Protection Officer, overlapping sanctions; (c) for each point, whether the decree defers to the data-protection law, fills a gap it leaves, or could conflict — and how the conflict is resolved; (d) recommended coordination mechanisms — joint subordinate regulation, a memorandum of understanding between the operating authority and the data-protection authority, joint enforcement; (e) the risk if the data-protection law changes during drafting and the forward-compatible choices that mitigate it. Mark every reference to a specific article of [country X]'s law as [confirm]. Output: the Cover Note, then the Two-Track Memo.
