## The safeguard, handed back as the learner's next action

The screen flags risks from the description only, and a flagged duplication is sometimes justified — a project may have a real reason the shared block cannot serve it. Use the output to open the conversation with the project and the EA Board, not to auto-reject; the Board makes the call, not the model.
